1

Foundation

Before any department, get the workspace itself right

Invite your team Ondi

Every teammate gets their own identity and a role — Users, SSO & Providers, Sessions & Security, Login Activity all live here now, not in each app.

Company Information & Localization Settings → General

Legal name, timezone, currency — this is what every invoice, report and date picker in the platform inherits from.

Branding Settings → General

Logo and accent colour — carries through every app's title, every PDF, every portal a customer sees.

Modules & Extensions Settings → General

Turn on the departments this workspace actually needs — the rest of this guide only matters for what's switched on here.

2

Finance

FinOps — the backbone every other department eventually bills through

Chart of Accounts /finance/accounts/chart-of-accounts

The ledger structure everything else — invoices, bills, payroll postings — ultimately lands in.

Tax Codes /finance/tax-codes

VAT and other rates, defined once, reused on every quotation, invoice and PO from here on.

Payment Gateways Settings → Finance

14 real options — Stripe, M‑Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money, Selcom, HaloPesa and more — connect whichever this business actually collects through.

Invoice / Quotation / PO numbering Settings → Finance → Invoices

Prefix, padding, starting number — the first document you send should already look like it came from an established business.

Receivables & Payables are now live FinOps

Invoices, delivery notes, POs, bills, vendors and expenses all draw on the setup above — this is where day‑to‑day finance work actually starts.

3

CRM

Bring in who you already sell to and sell toward

Import customers & partners /crm/customers/bulk-upload

Bring an existing customer list in as one batch rather than typing each one in by hand.

Build your pipeline /crm/leads

Leads carry a stage, priority and source out of the box — set the stages that match how this team actually sells before the first real lead lands.

4

Operations

ClearOS, CargoTracker & HuduFreight — where the actual freight moves

Set default workflows per shipment mode /studio/clearance

A workflow assigned per Freight Mode × Consignment Type — Sea, Air, Road, Rail, Multimodal against Import, Export, Transit, Re‑Export, Warehousing — so every new shipment case starts on the right track automatically.

Carriers & rate cards CargoTracker

The rates operations will quote and book against — get these in before the first real booking, not after.

Fleet & tracking HuduFreight Reports: Scale+

Vehicles and GPS tracking for anything moved with your own fleet rather than a subcontracted carrier.

5

People

NexusHR — everyone on the team, formally

Departments /hrm/departments

The structure employee records, approvals and reporting all hang off.

Employees /hrm/employees

Beyond the Ondi login each teammate already has — their actual HR record: role, department, employment details.

Leave policies /hrm/leaves

Leave types and entitlements, set once, before the first request comes in rather than adjudicated ad hoc.

Payroll /hrm/payroll

The last HR step, and the one everything above feeds — run it once everyone above is in the system correctly.

6

Compliance

ComplyOS — the regulatory side of the same business

Obligations calendar /complyos/obligations

Recurring regulatory deadlines this business is actually on the hook for.

Licence catalogue /complyos/license-catalog

Every licence this business needs to hold — the reference the Applications tracker checks itself against.

Applications /complyos/applications

Anything currently in progress against that catalogue, tracked to completion rather than in someone's inbox.

7

Automate

Studio — once every department above is real, connect them

Workflow templates, per app /studio/templates

ClearOS, SEAL, FinOps, Bliss, HuduFreight, CargoTracker and NexusHR each have their own automation surface — this is the last step precisely because it's most useful once there's real data everywhere else to trigger on.

✓

Fully live.

Every department has real data instead of empty states, the team is invited and roled, and automations are running across the departments that need them — this is what "fully set up" actually means on Hudumika.

Worth knowing before you build this for real
There's no getting‑started widget on WorkspaceHome today — a new admin currently lands on a plain "Recently viewed / My workspaces" grid with nothing pointing them at phase 1. This guide is designed to become that missing piece. Also: the pricing step's "compare all features" table quotes tier names (HuduStarter/HuduPlus/Hudu Advanced) that don't match the platform's real live packages (Starter/Growth/Scale/Enterprise, per 063_reprice_4_tiers.sql) — worth reconciling separately before this guide references tier names anywhere a customer can see them.
