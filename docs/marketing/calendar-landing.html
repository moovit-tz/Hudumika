What's underneath

Built for the way your day actually runs

Shareable booking links

A public page at /book/your-slug with your own durations, buffers, and working hours.

Bliss WebRTC video

Every meeting gets a real video room, not a placeholder link — host controls, waiting room, and recording included.

Two-way sync

Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, and iCal stay current in both directions — nothing double-booked, nothing stale.

Team free/busy overlay

A "meet with…" panel that overlays several calendars at once, so the open slot is obvious instead of guessed at.

Recurring events & reminders

Set a pattern once. Drag to reschedule, resize to adjust, and let reminders fire on their own from then on.

Tenant-isolated by design

Every calendar sits behind the same row-level tenant isolation the rest of Hudumika runs on — nothing crosses between workspaces.
