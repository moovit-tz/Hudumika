05 / Technical architecture

A modular platform.
A disciplined business core.

Evolve the existing React + Fastify + Kysely + Postgres monorepo as a modular monolith, with separate workers where justified. Product brands and subscription packages are not service boundaries.

Shared does not mean monolithic logic.

Finance owns journals and fiscal controls. Inventory owns commercial movements and valuation. Procurement owns purchase orders. A workshop owns service jobs. Integrate through contracts and application services, not arbitrary cross-domain table writes.

Extract services when evidence calls for it.

Tracking ingestion, provider connectors and AI/report workers may scale independently. Extract a business service for a proven isolation, ownership, scale or release need—not simply because it has a product logo.

07 / Recommended billing

One business base.
One price per industry.

A predictable hybrid: a base subscription with included users, industry packs with included operating units, and explicit consumption only where costs vary materially.

Recurring bill = Business Base + industry packs + extra users + extra operating units + optional upgrades + approved usage.
THE FOUNDATION

Business Base

Workspace Core + ERP Core, a defined legal-entity allowance, included business users, storage and standard support. Charge once per contracted workspace scope, regardless of industry count.

THE SPECIALIZATION

Industry subscription

Retail, Automotive or a relevant Logistics configuration. Include complete workflows and initial operating units. Do not add a bill for each app icon inside a purchased pack.

THE REAL COST

Transparent extras

Additional locations, licensed users, advanced FinOps, storage, AI and messaging. Quote hardware, migration, implementation and training separately.

09 / Billing that survives reality

The contract must match
what the software enforces.

Resolve overlap deliberately

Union shared capability grants; do not sum overlapping access. Retail + Automotive pays one base and one selected advanced FinOps upgrade, plus two specialist packs. Credit or retire redundant standalone add-ons.

Payments are not entitlements

Verified provider events or reconciled receipts update the billing ledger. A versioned subscription change updates access. Never grant a product because the browser reports payment success. Keep provider adapters neutral until a real supported provider is selected.

One proration policy

Snapshot prices, tax, currency and effective dates. Upgrades can be immediate; downgrades normally take effect at renewal after impact review. Define annual/anniversary rules, rounding, credits and refunds once; do not apply month-only proration blindly to annual contracts.

Meter committed facts

Use durable unique event IDs, correction entries, explicit periods and reconciliation. Never bill button clicks or retries. AI/SMS/telemetry need clear allowances, alerts and opt-in overages; retain idempotency beyond provider retry windows.

11 / Staged execution

Prove the model.
Then expand the industries.

Progress by acceptance gates. A few complete solutions will establish the platform more effectively than a large catalogue of partially connected apps.

01
Stabilize and define

Make the foundation trustworthy

Close audit blockers; map domain owners; define tenant/company/billing boundaries; classify capability readiness. Interview target customers and specify Tuma/Gereji. Exit: approved portfolio ADR and verified financial/stock invariants.

02
Compose and protect contracts

Build the commercial control plane

Version solution grants, allocations and changes. Prove one-base and deduplicated-license rules. Shadow-run new invoices against legacy contracts. Exit: no unexplained billing/access differences and replay-safe payment/meter tests.

03
Ship the flagship

Launch Hudumika for Logistics

Use existing investment to prove the case-to-cash journey. Release accepted ClearOS/SEAL/HuduFreight configurations; add Tuma when dispatch and delivery evidence are accepted. Exit: reference customers complete workflows, support diagnoses failures and recovery is demonstrated.

04
Prove reusable ERP

Retail, then Gereji-led Automotive

Retail proves common stock, POS, purchasing, returns and margin. Automotive adds vehicle/service-job intelligence. Use a parts retailer with a workshop as the multi-industry reference customer. Exit: reconciled sales/stock/cash, operator permissions and mixed-pack billing.

05
Scale distribution

Publish the industry blueprint

Each sector needs an owner, capability map, acceptance suite, migration, pricing unit, regulatory review where applicable and support playbook. Introduce partners later, with upgrade-safe extensions rather than customer forks. Exit: repeatable solution delivery.

Sequence can change with signed demand and readiness. Estimate staffing, integrations and remediation before committing dates. These are gates, not a promised delivery calendar.
12 / Architecture decision record

The decision to carry forward.

Proposed · Not yet adopted

One platform, composed ERP and industry solutions

Context: Hudumika already has shared identity, operational apps, grants and billing. Expansion risks duplicate ledgers, confusing plans and one separate stack per industry.

Decision: retain Hudumika as masterbrand; Workspace as the shared experience; ERP and Industries as business product families. Use versioned capabilities and one commercial control plane. Evolve the modular monolith.

Consequences: customers expand across sectors without migrating between platforms. Shared domains need stronger contracts and ownership. Catalogue migration adds short-term work; sector-native UX and support still require investment.

Deciders: product/brand owner, platform architect, finance/billing owner, industry leads and security/operations owner.

Should every industry get a database or microservice?

No. Keep product packaging independent of deployment. Start with modules, tenant isolation and company scope. Offer dedicated deployment for contracted residency, isolation, scale or operations needs, retaining the same code and capability contracts.

Should FinOps become the whole ERP?

FinOps owns the finance experience. ERP is the umbrella; Finance, Stock, Commerce, People and Procurement remain owned domains. Modularize existing finance-hosted capabilities incrementally. A navigation change must not create another ledger.

Can customers buy ClearOS or Gereji directly?

Yes. Offer a focused entry point and quote that includes minimum Workspace/ERP dependencies. Expansion reuses the same tenant and transparently converts overlapping charges.

What happens to existing subscribers?

Preserve plan IDs, historical invoices and contractual access. Map packages to versioned grants, shadow-run billing and review migration with customers. New display names must not silently remove features, raise contracted prices or rewrite invoices.

What does “unlimited apps” mean?

If used, it means no separate app charge for capabilities explicitly included in the purchased edition. It does not mean unlimited users, sites, storage, AI, external services, every industry, third-party products or every future major release.
