Hudumika — registration names & activities

Registering “Hudumika” in Tanzania as a brand / trade name, owned by Moovit Mobility Company.

Structure: Moovit Mobility Company is the parent/owner; Hudumika is the product brand and operating company — the same relationship as Anthropic → Claude. In Tanzania this would normally be a locally incorporated limited company (with Moovit as shareholder) that trades under the Hudumika brand.

10 name suggestions to register

  1. Hudumika Limited
    Simplest — brand and company name are the same.
  2. Hudumika Technologies Limited
    Clear software/technology identity.
  3. Hudumika Tanzania Limited
    Signals the local entity of a wider group.
  4. Hudumika Digital Services Limited
    Fits the “services” meaning of the word.
  5. Hudumika Software Solutions Limited
    Descriptive; good for licensing and contracts.
  6. Hudumika Cloud Limited
    Emphasises the hosted platform.
  7. Hudumika Logistics Technology Limited
    Fits the customs, freight and fleet core.
  8. Hudumika Business Systems Limited
    Covers the finance / HR / CRM suite.
  9. Hudumika Africa Limited
    Room to expand beyond Tanzania.
  10. Hudumika by Moovit trade name
    Brand line to use with the company name (not a company name itself).

“Hudumika” comes from the Swahili huduma (service). Check each name for availability on the BRELA online registration system before filing. A company / business name (BRELA) and a trademark (BRELA Industrial Property) are separate registrations — register the trademark for “Hudumika” and its logo too, otherwise a company name alone does not protect the brand. This is a planning list, not legal advice — confirm with a Tanzanian advocate.

Business activities (short)

  • Developing, licensing and hosting the Hudumika business platform (software-as-a-service, subscriptions).
  • Customs clearing, freight and shipment-tracking software; fleet and transport management.
  • Finance and accounting software: invoicing, bills, expenses, tax returns, bank reconciliation.
  • HR, payroll and workforce management software; e-signature and document workflows.
  • Cloud file storage, business email, calendar and team communication.
  • CRM, contacts and customer-support tools; bulk and transactional SMS messaging.
  • Identity and access management (single sign-on) and a developer API / integrations.
  • AI assistants and automation built into the platform.
  • Implementation, training, data migration and support for business customers.

Some activities need separate approval before launch — e.g. payment or wallet services (Bank of Tanzania), SMS sender IDs (TCRA), and personal-data handling (Personal Data Protection Commission registration). Also confirm foreign-ownership rules and whether a TIC investment certificate applies.
