## Remaining blockers and discrepancies

### P1 — A fully paid USD invoice still leaves a large GL receivable

**Fresh reproduction:** create an issued USD 100 invoice at exchange rate 2650 in a TZS tenant, then record a USD 100 receipt. Both API operations succeed. The invoice creates a 265,000 debit to account 1100, but the receipt credits only 100. The remaining GL receivable is **264,900**, despite full payment of the document.

`invoices.routes.ts:1284` adds FX metadata, but `1295–1297` still post Number(amount), clearAmount and excessAmount without conversion. GLService.post stores those numbers; metadata does not convert them. Credit-note posting likewise attaches FX metadata to unconverted amounts. Bill posting/payment also requires a complete consistent reporting-currency policy rather than metadata-only changes.

Invoice issuance conversion is additionally conditional on fx > 1 (`invoices.routes.ts:949`, `1094`), which skips legitimate rates below one. The new FX tests cover issuance with 2650 and local TZS amounts; they do not cover receipt settlement, foreign-currency credits, non-TZS reporting currencies or sub-unit rates.

Required: one consistent original/reporting currency representation, applied across issuance, settlement, credits, reversals and reports; permanent tests asserting zero remaining receivable after full settlement and matching subledgers to the GL.

### P1 — Credit limits are not credit allocation

Credit issuance still only posts the journal and appends invoice activity. Invoice outstanding/payment calculations and GLService aged receivables do not consume the linked credits. The cumulative cap therefore prevents over-crediting in selected cases but does not resolve the original invoice/aging/ledger mismatch.

The new stats field also does not fully resolve currency aggregation: credit-notes.routes.ts:140 still computes totalCredited by adding every currency bucket, and CreditNotes.tsx still consumes totalCredited for its total card.

Required: apply credits consistently to invoice outstanding balances, statements and aging, including paid invoices/refunds and voids; migrate the metric consumer to currency-separated data. Test end-to-end balances rather than only credit creation/rejection.

### P1 — Bank matching still accepts unequal amounts

**Fresh reproduction:** a statement amount of 999 was successfully matched to a same-tenant bank journal line with debit 100. HTTP 200 was returned. The route selects debit, credit and currency but does not compare them to the statement amount or enforce currency/date suitability. It only rejects VOIDED rather than requiring an explicitly eligible posting state.

The uniqueness migration and tenant validation are real improvements. They do not prove accounting reconciliation. Review the intended amount/currency/date policy, correct bank-balance evidence and behavior under concurrent match/unmatch requests.

### Verification/reporting gap

The updated readiness report says the atomicity test verifies absence of both the credit record and journal, but its assertion only checks the credit_notes rows. The suite also has setup cases counted among its 14 tests and lacks independent failure-injection cases for asset acquisition and intercompany posting. Tests can pass while the remaining receipt and reconciliation defects persist.

Do not use the green suite as certification of the broader claims. Add the missing permanent assertions and update the report to distinguish fixed subcases from unresolved transaction families.

## Work unchanged from the earlier review

The reviewed FinOps shell/report paths still need lazy route splitting, bounded expense/journal/legacy report reads and exports, date-only cash-flow month boundaries, POS retry concurrency protection, enforcement of tax preparation at close/export actions, broader employee claims and permission acceptance. Provider sandbox testing, unsupported QuickBooks AP adapters, accountant sign-off, measured capacity, operational monitoring and restore/failover remain separate release gates.

Prioritize the reproducible monetary defects, finish credit allocation and bank matching rules, strengthen regression assertions, then complete staging acceptance. The progress is substantial, but the release decision remains **not approved for unrestricted production use**.
