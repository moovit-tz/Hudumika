## P1 — Simultaneous POS accounting retries create duplicate journals

Endpoint: POST /v1/finance/pos/sales/:id/retry-posting (pos.routes.ts:509 onward).

The route reads the failed sale, payments and existing journal in one transaction, calls postSaleJournal outside that transaction, then updates the sale in a separate transaction. Both callers can observe no journal before either posts.

Fresh disposable-tenant reproduction: seed one POSTING_FAILED sale for TZS 100 and its CASH payment; submit two simultaneous retry requests. Coordinate entry into GLService.post with a short test-only barrier, then execute real GL posting for both. Responses are 200 and 422; **two POSTED AR journal entries exist for the same sale**, expected one. Rejecting the second final status update does not roll back its already committed journal. The extra journal duplicates cash/revenue posting.

Required: atomically claim/lock the sale and make journal creation plus completion one protected operation, or enforce equivalent database-backed idempotency. A sequential existing-journal lookup alone is insufficient. Add permanent concurrency tests asserting one journal and a consistent sale state after simultaneous retries, including failures/restarts.

The permanent suite's section named POS keyless duplicate guard tests invoices/:id/payment, not this POS retry endpoint. Its passing cases do not establish the POS retry invariant.
