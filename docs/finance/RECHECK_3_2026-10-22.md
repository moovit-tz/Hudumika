## Remaining findings

### P1 — A linked credit may use another FX rate without clearing historical AR

`apps/api/src/routes/credit-notes.routes.ts:213` permits `body.exchange_rate` to override the source invoice rate. Lines 280–296 post the credit using that rate, without a historical-AR clearing/realized-FX adjustment policy.

Fresh HTTP/database reproduction:
1. Issue USD 100 at rate 2650 in a TZS-reporting tenant: AR debit 265,000.
2. Issue a full linked USD 100 credit with exchange_rate 1: accepted, HTTP 201.
3. Credit posts AR credit 100, leaving **264,900** on the combined invoice/credit GL receivable while document-level credit allocation consumes the full USD invoice.

A full linked credit must clear the source's reporting-currency receivable. Either constrain linked credits to the source valuation or explicitly account for the FX difference under an agreed policy. Rejecting the inconsistent rate is also a valid control; silently accepting an unreconciled residual is not. Add permanent source-to-GL tests for same-rate and changed-rate full/partial credits, settlement and voids.

### P2 — Fully credited invoice status stays Unpaid

Credit-note creation appends invoice activity (`credit-notes.routes.ts:300–304`) but does not recompute the linked sales invoice status. Void likewise updates the credit record without recalculating invoice settlement status.

Fresh reproduction: issue TZS 100, then post a full linked TZS 100 credit. Source invoice status remains **Unpaid**, expected **Credited**. Corrected outstanding/aging calculations now report no balance, so status and balance disagree. This can mislead status-filtered registers and collection workflows. Recompute status under the source invoice lock on credit issue/void, including partially paid and partially credited invoices.
