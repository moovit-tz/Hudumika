What it does

A clearing agent's whole back office, in one login.

Hudumika grew from a customs-clearance system (ClearOS) into a full operating layer for the businesses around it — the same tenant that lodges a TANSAD declaration also runs its payroll, its helpdesk and its sales pipeline through the same platform, with one identity provider and one set of books underneath every app.

What it captures

Real operational records, not form submissions.

Every module writes into the same tenant-scoped Postgres schema. A sample of what actually gets stored, by domain:

Customs & freight

  • DeclarationsTANSAD numbers, HS codes, duty/VAT assessment, lane and release status
  • Origin & riskEAC/AfCFTA rules-of-origin certificates, dangerous-goods class, sanctions-screening hits
  • ShipmentsAWB/BL number, container number, vessel/voyage, ETA/ETD, stage history
  • Depot & releaseEquipment interchange receipts, release orders, delivery notes

Finance

  • LedgerJournal entries, trial balance, GL period close
  • AR / APSales invoices, supplier bills, credit notes, recurring invoices
  • Assets & budgetsFixed-asset register with depreciation, department budgets vs. actuals
  • CashBank reconciliation lines, FX rates, petty-cash deposit/disbursement approvals

People

  • AttendanceBiometric-device clock-in/out timestamps, live clocked-in status
  • WorkforceShift rosters, leave requests and balances, payroll runs
  • RecruitmentPipeline stage per candidate, interview notes
  • IdentityPasskey/2FA enrollment, government-ID verification level, session devices

Customer engagement

  • SupportTickets and every message, across WhatsApp, email, SMS and in-app channels
  • Calls & meetingsDuration, participants, host controls, AI meeting summaries
  • SalesLeads, deals, quotations, per-customer agreed contract pricing
  • CommsSMS sends and delivery receipts, opt-outs, calendar bookings

Documents & e-signature

  • Execution recordsOrdinary e-sign, witnessed signature, sworn affidavit or full notarial certification — on the same document, escalating as needed rather than a second app
  • Chain of custodyEvery view/sign/decline/void as an append-only audit event; a tamper-evident hash anchored to Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps
  • Jurisdiction statusWhich execution types are legally supported per country, cited to the actual statute — Tanzania populated from primary-source research, not invented
  • VerificationA scannable QR + short code that resolves to a public, real-time status/hash check — no login required

Gaps & roadmap

What's actually missing, not a rounding error.

The real, specific gaps behind each percentage above — not "more features," but named items with a reason they're not done. Two are hard floors that no amount of engineering effort closes on its own.

Sign

90% → 100%
  • Ondi OTP bypass — deliberately declined: would silently weaken a per-envelope security control a sender explicitly turned on. A permanent 10%, by design.
  • Jurisdiction coverage — Tanzania alone has real, cited legal research; Kenya/Uganda/Rwanda are schema-ready but legally unreviewed. Same primary-source method, 3 more countries.
  • No independent security certification — SOC 2 / eIDAS-equivalent is an audit process, not code.
  • No bulk-send / mail-merge — one envelope, one document, at a time.

HuduBI

60% → 100%
  • Cost & unit economics (M4) — not unbuilt, unbuildable: zero real infrastructure-cost data exists anywhere in the platform to compute it from. Needs a tenant entering real provider rates, or a real billing-API integration, before this is an engineering task at all.
  • No self-serve query builder — the real one that exists is SUPER_ADMIN-only.
  • No natural-language insight, scheduled reports, or ad-hoc drill-down — today's dashboards are curated, not explorable.
  • "AI Overview" / "AI Models & Predictions" pages unverified — the two files carrying HuduBI's actual "AI" promise weren't touched or confirmed this pass.

FinOps

95% → 100%
  • Payroll → GL — now wired (verified 10 Sep): POST /v1/payroll/runs/:id/mark-paid posts a real journal entry — gross pay, PAYE payable, statutory contributions payable and matched employer cost as separate lines (migration 294). The earlier "not closed" flag is resolved.
  • No consolidated multi-entity statements at group scale.
  • No external audit-firm API.

ClearOS

95% → 100%
  • No live customs API anywhere in the region — confirmed by direct research: Tanzania/Kenya/Uganda/Rwanda customs authorities are portal-only. External infrastructure gap, not a Hudumika one.
  • Limited global carrier/EDI network — only Maersk has a real public API integrated.

Projects

85% → 100%
  • Working today, unchanged — project list, kanban/table board, Gantt with dependencies, milestones, timesheets, per-project invoicing & retainers, discussions, activity log. This is the 85%.
  • "Project OS" enterprise layer merged but not wired — portfolios/programs, WBS & phases, earned-value cost control, risk/issue/change registers, an approval engine, procurement and 14 industry packs landed on master, but every /v1/project-os endpoint returns a 500: the routes, services and generated DB types were written against a schema that was never migrated. Fix is bounded — one corrective migration, regenerate the DB types from the live schema, add member-scoped authorization — but until then none of it runs. Full breakdown in docs/project-os-hardening-review.md.
  • No portfolio/program access model — the new hierarchy is tenant-wide only; any project member can read every portfolio's budgets and every other project's risk register.
  • One fabricated metric to remove — the enterprise Command Center hardcodes portfolio SPI to 1.02 and shows three placeholder DORA numbers in the DevOps pack.

NexusHR

80% → 100%
  • Data-populated live verification not done — every route was hit live and all resolve, enforce the entitlement and pass RLS, but the dev DB has no fully seeded HR tenant, so a click-through of every screen against real staff/payroll/attendance rows (the thing that took Sign to 90%) is still outstanding. This is the bulk of the remaining 20%.
  • AI insights need a key — the HR-digest endpoint and card are built; a tenant must supply its own Anthropic API key (Settings ▸ Integrations ▸ AI) before it returns anything.
  • Voice/video is provisioning, not code — 1:1 and group meetings (mesh WebRTC, breakout rooms, host controls) already run through Bliss; production needs a TURN server for strict-NAT clients and Redis-backed presence for multi-node.
  • Minor — a dead /v1/hr/org-chart deprecation shim (the page correctly uses /v1/org-chart); payroll page still on hardcoded hex rather than design tokens.

Contacts

82% → 100%
  • Data-populated live verification not done — every route was exercised end-to-end on 10 September (CRUD, fuzzy dedup + merge, nested labels with cycle guards, smart-group membership, CSV/vCard round-trip, cross-tenant isolation — all pass), but against test data the run created itself; there is no fully seeded Contacts tenant for a screen-by-screen click-through against hundreds of real contacts, labels and merge history. Same ceiling as NexusHR.
  • Google / Outlook contact sync is inert until configured — the OAuth code path is complete and fails honestly ("set them in Settings ▸ Integrations"); each tenant must supply its own Google/Microsoft Client ID & Secret before a sync runs. Same class as the BYO AI key.
  • No interactive import field-mapping — CSV/vCard import auto-maps a fixed set of column aliases (Google, Outlook and generic headers); an unusual column header is silently dropped rather than offered for mapping.
  • Frontend not re-checked visually this pass — the API and workflow layer is proven; the React screens were not re-verified against it (no browser in this environment).

Drive

80% → 100%
  • Object storage, malware scanning and Office preview are code-complete but not provisioned — the S3-compatible backend (real Signature V4 signing, no SDK dependency), ClamAV upload scanning and LibreOffice document-preview conversion all degrade honestly to their previous/disk behaviour when unconfigured; none has been run against a live external service in this environment. Same "real once configured" shape as SMTP/Meta/QuickBooks elsewhere on this page.
  • Only OneDrive has a real third-party sync — a genuine per-tenant BYO Microsoft Graph OAuth connection (consent, token refresh, recursive file listing verified live). Dropbox and Box are now an honest "not supported yet" instead of a silent mock connect; Mega has no standard OAuth2 to build against at all.
  • No browser click-through of the rebuilt viewer — the new inline CSV/text rendering, the office-preview fallback panel, and the connector-setup dialog are type-checked and logic-verified against the live API, but not visually confirmed (no browser tool this session).
  • Data-populated verification not done — same ceiling as NexusHR/Contacts: no fully seeded Drive tenant with hundreds of real files/folders/shares to click through.
P0
Give HuduBI real self-serve exploration.

It's the furthest behind its category leader (Looker/Power BI) of any app in the platform, and because every other app's metrics flow through it, closing this gap compounds value everywhere else rather than adding one more feature in isolation. Verify "AI Overview"/"AI Models" first — confirm what's real before building on top of it.

P1
Close the flagged production-tier gaps.

Sign's KE/UG/RW jurisdiction research and reconciling the merged Projects "Project OS" layer against a real schema are both bounded, well-understood tasks sitting inside already-production apps — higher leverage than adding depth to an active-development app. The Projects one is also the only item on this page that regressed a working app on merge, so it carries the most urgency. (FinOps payroll→GL, previously listed here, is done.)

P2
Everything else, roughly in tier order.

Ondi SCIM/adaptive MFA, Bliss contact-center depth, CRM marketing automation — genuine gaps, but the apps are already functional for the core East Africa logistics/clearing use case. The regional customs-API gap belongs to government infrastructure, not an engineering backlog — track it, don't sprint on it.

Architecture

One tenant, one wall.

🔒

Every tenant's data sits behind genuine Postgres Row-Level Security — the API connects as a restricted, non-superuser role (hudumika_app) with every RLS-enabled table under FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY, so a query missing its tenant scope is rejected by the database itself, not just filtered by application code. That's the floor; every route still scopes its own queries explicitly on top of it.
