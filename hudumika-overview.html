What it does

A clearing agent's whole back office, in one login.

Hudumika grew from a customs-clearance system (ClearOS) into a full operating layer for the businesses around it — the same tenant that lodges a TANSAD declaration also runs its payroll, its helpdesk and its sales pipeline through the same platform, with one identity provider and one set of books underneath every app.

What it captures

Real operational records, not form submissions.

Every module writes into the same tenant-scoped Postgres schema. A sample of what actually gets stored, by domain:

Customs & freight

  • DeclarationsTANSAD numbers, HS codes, duty/VAT assessment, lane and release status
  • Origin & riskEAC/AfCFTA rules-of-origin certificates, dangerous-goods class, sanctions-screening hits
  • ShipmentsAWB/BL number, container number, vessel/voyage, ETA/ETD, stage history
  • Depot & releaseEquipment interchange receipts, release orders, delivery notes

Finance

  • LedgerJournal entries, trial balance, GL period close
  • AR / APSales invoices, supplier bills, credit notes, recurring invoices
  • Assets & budgetsFixed-asset register with depreciation, department budgets vs. actuals
  • CashBank reconciliation lines, FX rates, petty-cash deposit/disbursement approvals

People

  • AttendanceBiometric-device clock-in/out timestamps, live clocked-in status
  • WorkforceShift rosters, leave requests and balances, payroll runs
  • RecruitmentPipeline stage per candidate, interview notes
  • IdentityPasskey/2FA enrollment, government-ID verification level, session devices

Customer engagement

  • SupportTickets and every message, across WhatsApp, email, SMS and in-app channels
  • Calls & meetingsDuration, participants, host controls, AI meeting summaries
  • SalesLeads, deals, quotations, per-customer agreed contract pricing
  • CommsSMS sends and delivery receipts, opt-outs, calendar bookings

Documents & e-signature

  • Execution recordsOrdinary e-sign, witnessed signature, sworn affidavit or full notarial certification — on the same document, escalating as needed rather than a second app
  • Chain of custodyEvery view/sign/decline/void as an append-only audit event; a tamper-evident hash anchored to Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps
  • Jurisdiction statusWhich execution types are legally supported per country, cited to the actual statute — Tanzania populated from primary-source research, not invented
  • VerificationA scannable QR + short code that resolves to a public, real-time status/hash check — no login required

Architecture

One tenant, one wall.

🔒

Every tenant's data sits behind genuine Postgres Row-Level Security — the API connects as a restricted, non-superuser role (hudumika_app) with every RLS-enabled table under FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY, so a query missing its tenant scope is rejected by the database itself, not just filtered by application code. That's the floor; every route still scopes its own queries explicitly on top of it.
