What changes, architecturally
Account creation moves out of each workspace's own onboarding and becomes a single call into Ondi. Everything downstream is just a grant on top of an identity that already exists:
  • Enterprise signup = create identity (if new) → create workspace → grant that identity the ADMIN role on it.
  • Team invite = grant an existing or new identity a role on an existing workspace — never a second password.
  • Personal signup = create identity → no workspace grant required at all, or a lightweight workspace‑of‑one.
  • Login happens once, at Ondi — every workspace after that is a switch, not a sign‑in.
