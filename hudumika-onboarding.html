Behind the scenes
All six steps are gathered client‑side into a single draft, then submitted in one call — POST /v1/onboarding/complete — which creates the tenant, the owner's account, the plan assignment and the payment record together. On success, the new admin is logged straight in and lands inside their own workspace — no separate confirmation email or second login step.
