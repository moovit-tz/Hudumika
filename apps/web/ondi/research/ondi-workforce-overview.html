Ondi Workforce is structured as eight deeply integrated modules, each independently valuable, compounding in power as an end-to-end workforce identity operating system.
🗂

The Workforce Directory is the canonical identity record for every person who interacts with the organisation — employees, contractors, interns, consultants, vendors, and partners — with org hierarchy, reporting lines, department structure, and real-time employment status.

Org HierarchyReporting lines & structureDynamic org chart with reporting lines, team structure, and branch/location assignments. Auto-updates on promotions, transfers, and departures.
Identity TypesMulti-worker-class supportEmployees · Contractors · Interns · Consultants · Vendors · Partners — each with appropriate access scope, trust level, and compliance rules.
Device & Asset GraphIdentity-bound assetsLaptops, phones, SIM cards, biometric devices, and company IDs bound to the employee identity record. Auto-flags on offboarding.
Directory IntegrationsSaaS sync layerGoogle Workspace · Microsoft 365 · Slack · Notion · Jira · GitHub · AWS IAM · Azure AD · ERP systems. Bi-directional SCIM sync.

2 — Lifecycle Engine
Automated Joiner · Mover · Leaver workflows — the heart of the product.
The Lifecycle Engine orchestrates the full employment lifecycle in a single automated flow — triggered by HR events and executed across all connected systems simultaneously. No manual IT tickets. No forgotten accounts.
PHASETRIGGERAUTOMATED ACTIONS

MOVERRole or department changeRevoke old permissions → grant new permissions → update reporting lines → adjust compliance scope → recalculate risk/access level. Zero access gap or access bleed.

Every company device is bound to an employee identity record — laptops, phones, SIM cards, biometric access devices, and physical company IDs. On offboarding, all device access is revoked, unreturned assets are flagged, and supported devices can be remotely wiped.
ASSET TYPETRACKINGOFFBOARDING ACTION
Laptops & WorkstationsDevice trust + MDM bindingCertificate-based device trust. MDM integration for policy enforcement. Remote wipe on exit. Automatic SSO revocation on device deregistration.
Mobile DevicesBYOD & company-issuedContainerised company access on personal devices. Full wipe for company-issued. MFA device deregistration on exit.
SIM Cards & Physical IDsPhysical asset registrySIM and physical ID assignment tracked in identity record. Flagged as unreturned on offboarding. Blocks re-issue until confirmed return.
Biometric Access DevicesPhysical access gatesBiometric (fingerprint/face) door access bound to workforce identity. Revoked instantly on offboarding. Covers offices, data centres, and secured zones.

04 — Trust-Aware Workforce Identity
Workforce Trust Intelligence — Ondi's Unique Moat
Unlike Okta or JumpCloud, Ondi Workforce layers a live Trust Score, KYC verification, and behavioural identity signals on top of every access decision — making access risk-aware, not just role-aware.
🔐 What Makes Ondi Different
Others: Identity access management — who are you, what role do you have?
Ondi: Identity + Trust + Compliance + Workforce Lifecycle — who are you, how trustworthy is your identity, are you compliant, and what is your current risk level?

Every access decision in Ondi Workforce is informed by a live Trust Score combining: Identity authenticity (35%) · Behavioural consistency (25%) · Financial patterns (25%) · Compliance standing (15%).
Adaptive Access by Trust & Risk Scenario
✅ High Trust Employee
Score ≥ 700 · Low Risk
Seamless passwordless access. 1-tap authentication. Minimum friction. No additional verification required.
⚠️ Impossible Travel
Anomaly Detected
Login from Nairobi at 9am, Dar es Salaam at 10am → automatic step-up MFA + security alert. Session held pending verification.
⚠️ Unusual Device
Unrecognised Device
First-time login from unregistered device → biometric step-up required. Device registration flow. Security team notified.
🔴 Repeated Failed Auth
Brute Force Signal
5+ consecutive failures → account temporarily locked. Manager and security team alerted. Unlock requires helpdesk + biometric verification.
🔴 Suspicious Behaviour
Insider Threat Signal
Bulk data export, off-hours privileged access, or unusual API key usage → temporary access restriction + immediate security alert + audit trail.
🔴 Dormant Identity
Ghost Employee Risk
60+ days no login → account flagged as dormant. Auto-notification to HR and manager for review. Can escalate to auto-disable.
