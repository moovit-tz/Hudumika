Shareable Booking Links

Cal.com style public booking pages (`/book/:slug`) with customized durations, buffer times, working hours, and guest questions.

Bliss WebRTC Video

Instant 1-click video call room links embedded directly into calendar invites with host controls, waiting rooms, and recording.

2-Way Sync

Keep your existing calendar up to date. Bi-directional sync with Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, and Apple iCal.
