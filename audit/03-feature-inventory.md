# Feature inventory

ClearOS exposes dashboard; shipment operations, creation, detail/edit; Duty Check; FCL, LCL, air and transit estimates; estimate history and public sharing; compliance overview/quick check/trade wizard/sanctions/origin; penalties; carbon; products/services; customs reference; clearing rate cards. Source: ClearOSShell.tsx and evidence/page-inventory.json.

Legacy declarations redirect to operations or SEAL; workflows to Studio; freight booking to CargoTracker; delivery documents to FinOps; depot to tracking. These are cross-app dependencies, not missing features merely because a redirect exists. End-to-end authorization and destination availability across every redirect remain NOT VERIFIED.

Operational API registration does not prove persistence, notification delivery, customs authority acceptance, or legal correctness. No real government integration was invented or contacted.
